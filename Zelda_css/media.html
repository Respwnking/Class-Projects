The Sound of Hyrule

Music is one of the most memorable parts of The Legend of Zelda. Overworld themes, town music, and dungeon tracks all help set the mood of each area.

In some games Link even uses instruments, such as the Ocarina of Time, to change the time of day, open secret passages, or travel quickly around the map.

Why the Music Matters

  • It tells you when danger is near or when you are safe.
  • It helps each location feel unique and easy to remember.
  • It makes emotional scenes more powerful and memorable.
  • It connects players across different generations of games.

Zelda Timeline

Zelda’s timeline splits into three paths after Ocarina of Time: Hero is Defeated Timeline Leads to classics like A Link to the Past and Link’s Awakening. Hero is Victorious – Child Timeline Link goes back in time, warning Zelda, leading to games like Majora’s Mask and Twilight Princess. Hero is Victorious – Adult Timeline Link disappears from the future, leading to Wind Waker and its sequels. All Zelda games fit into one of these three branches.
