Heroes, Princesses, and Villains

Across the Zelda timeline, many versions of Link, Zelda, and Ganon appear. Even though their worlds and stories change, their roles remain tied together by the Triforce: courage, wisdom, and power.

Link

Link represents the Triforce of Courage. He is a brave warrior who rises to defend Hyrule in every era. Sometimes he starts as a simple villager or knight-in-training, but destiny always leads him to the Master Sword.

Princess Zelda

Zelda holds the Triforce of Wisdom. Across games she appears as a princess, scholar, ninja-like Sheik, or powerful magic wielder. She guides Link and protects Hyrule from darkness.

Ganondorf / Ganon

Ganondorf is the primary villain of the series. He leads the Gerudo tribe but becomes corrupted by the desire for absolute power. In many timelines he transforms into his beast form, Ganon. https://zeldauniverse.net

Impa

Impa has many appearances throughout the series: sometimes as a warrior, other times as an elder guide. She protects the royal family and carries important Sheikah knowledge.

Zelda Character Facts

Link almost never speaks, allowing players to feel like they are the ones experiencing the adventure.

Zelda is often reincarnated just like Link, which explains why many versions of her appear in different centuries.

Ganondorf is the only male Gerudo born in 100 years, a major reason he rises to leadership before falling into darkness.
