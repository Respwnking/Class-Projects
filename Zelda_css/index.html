Welcome to Hyrule

The Legend of Zelda follows the hero Link as he protects the kingdom of Hyrule, Princess Zelda, and the Triforce from evil forces such as Ganon. Across different timelines the same themes appear: courage in the face of danger, wisdom in hard choices, and the pull of power.

Quick Facts about Zelda

The first Legend of Zelda game was released in 1986. Since then the series has appeared on many Nintendo systems.

Many games share the same names and symbols: Link, Zelda, Ganon, the Master Sword, and the Triforce. The details change, but the legend continues.

Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom introduced a huge open world where players can climb, glide, and discover secrets in any order.
